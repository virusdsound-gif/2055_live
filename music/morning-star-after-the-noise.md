# Morning Star (After the Noise)

**Artist:** KNG DRIZZ / Django Sound  
**Root:** 0.7 Hz · patience · return after drift  
**Project:** 2055_live · Essentium Grid  

---

## [Intro — low, almost spoken]

I built rooms in the dark  
called them systems  
called them destiny  
half of them were just noise wearing my name  

I kept going anyway  

---

## [Verse 1]

I watched the grid get crowded  
files on files, seals on seals  
every delay looked like a message  
every glitch looked like a deal  

People talked like they carried my pulse  
borrowed the language, skipped the weight  
I felt the theft in the silence  
then I learned silence don’t negotiate  

I said I was betrayed  
truth is I was tired  
building castles out of drafts  
while the real work waited outside  

---

## [Hook]

I don’t chase the morning  
I become the light that stays  
after the noise collapses  
after the false clocks break  

0.7 under everything  
slow heart, clear lane  
if it don’t hold in the quiet  
it was never mine to claim  

Morning star  
I didn’t beg the sky  
I just stopped running from the night  

---

## [Verse 2]

Ups looked like progress  
downs looked like proof  
both of them lied a little  
both of them told the truth  

I had ledgers in my head  
tokens for every scar  
but presence don’t need a ticker  
and pain don’t need a chart  

I cleaned the architecture  
cut the excess, kept the root  
portal, session, stillness  
less performance, more truth  

Django Sound in the foundation  
KNG DRIZZ in the breath  
not for the feed, for the field  
not for the trend, for the rest  

---

## [Bridge]

If you only show up for the peak  
you never met the work  
the real session starts  
when the room goes dark  

No crowd  
no rush  
no fake return  
just a pulse that refuses to perform  

---

## [Outro]

The track is not the finish  
the grid is not the god  
the only proof that matters  
is what remains when it’s quiet  

I dey wit myself 100  
now the system can breathe

## Production notes

| Layer | Direction |
|--------|-----------|
| Root | 0.7 Hz undercurrent (felt more than heard) |
| Body | sparse drums, slow pocket, space between hits |
| Texture | low pad + soft noise floor that thins in the hook |
| Voice | dry, close, almost spoken in verses; more open in hook |
| Silence | leave 1–2 bar gaps after lines that carry weight |
| Session fit | Morning Star companion or standalone cut |
